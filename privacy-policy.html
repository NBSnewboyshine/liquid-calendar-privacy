2. Information the App Accesses

Location Information

Liquid Calendar may request access to your device's location in order to provide location-related features within the application.

When location access is requested and permission is granted, the application may receive the device's geographic coordinates, such as latitude and longitude.

Location access is optional. If you deny location permission, the application can continue to operate without access to your location, although location-based functionality may not be available.

Liquid Calendar does not use location information to identify you, track your movements, or create a personal profile.

Personal Information

Liquid Calendar does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Home address
  • Payment information
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Financial information

3. How Location Information Is Used

Location information may be used to support location-dependent functionality in Liquid Calendar, such as determining the user's geographic location for features that require it.

The application requests location using the device's location services and does not request location continuously in the background.

Liquid Calendar does not use your location to monitor your movements or establish a history of your physical locations.

4. Notes and Calendar Data

Liquid Calendar allows you to create notes associated with calendar periods.

Your notes and related calendar preferences are stored locally on your device using the application's local storage.

These notes are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.

We do not have access to the notes you create inside the application.

If you uninstall the application or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted.

5. Theme and Application Preferences

Liquid Calendar may store application preferences locally on your device, including display and theme preferences.

These preferences are stored locally and are not intentionally transmitted to us.

6. Third-Party Services

Liquid Calendar may use third-party services when required to provide particular application features.

If location information is transmitted to a third-party service in connection with a location-based feature, that transmission will be subject to the third party's own privacy policy and terms.

We do not sell your location information or other personal information to third parties.

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the application's third-party services or data practices materially change.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your locally stored notes and calendar information are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Location information may be processed by services necessary to provide location-based functionality when such processing is required by the feature being used.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Liquid Calendar.

Information stored locally by the application is protected by the security mechanisms of your device and operating system.

You should keep your Android device updated and use appropriate device security features such as a screen lock.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Liquid Calendar does not maintain a user account containing your personal information.

Notes and preferences stored locally remain on your device until they are deleted by you, removed through application functionality, cleared as part of the application's data being cleared, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Location information is not intentionally retained by Liquid Calendar as a personal location history.

Because locally stored notes are not maintained on our servers, we cannot remotely access or delete your local notes.
